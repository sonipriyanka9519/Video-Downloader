Settings detail sheets & About
Every chevron row in Settings resolves here. Radio choices are sheets, not sub-screens, so a one-tap change never costs a navigation. Destructive rows get a dialog that names the real number.
Assumptions: selections apply immediately on tap and the sheet closes — no Save button. Download location and app language hand off to the system pickers, so they're not designed here.

DESIGN NOTES
• Radio rows are 60dp with a subtitle explaining the consequence in plain words — "Saves data and storage" beats the word "Smallest" alone.
• Selected row gets an accent-soft fill as well as the filled dot, so the choice is legible at a glance and not only by a 10px circle.
• Parallel downloads uses a three-up segmented control, not a radio list: three integers don't deserve three full rows.
• Confirm dialogs name the real count and state what survives, so the user isn't guessing whether downloads go too.
• About carries the honest disclaimer at the bottom in muted text — present for the store review, never in the user's way.
• The default-browser prompt is a sheet with an explicit "Not now" and never re-asks after two dismissals.
