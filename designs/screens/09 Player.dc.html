DESIGN NOTES
• Every control has a gesture twin, so the overlay can disappear after 3s without hiding functionality.
• Seek bar is the only accent element in the chrome — played portion red, remainder white 30%, 14dp thumb that grows on touch.
• Speed is a chip, not a menu: tap cycles 0.5→2x, long-press opens the list. Loop, subtitles and background audio live in the overflow.
• Queue inherits the library's current filter, so "next" always means what the user was just browsing. Autoplay toggle is pinned at the sheet top.
• Countdown is cancellable and shows the real title — never an unexplained auto-advance.
• Audio files reuse the same player with a music tile in place of video; identical controls, no separate audio screen.
OPEN QUESTION
Swipe down defaults to PiP rather than exit. That is the better retention behaviour but surprises people expecting "back". Keep PiP as default, or make exit the default with PiP on the icon only?
