SCREEN 06
Downloads — the library
The app's second home. One list holds transfers and finished files: in-flight items pinned under a DOWNLOADING heading, everything else under day headers. Search, sort, layout and filters live in the header and a single chip row — no sub-tabs.
Assumptions: thumbnails are placeholders regenerated from the finished file. Filenames come from smart naming (video title → page title → site · date), never "video player_NNNNN". The old floating "How to Download" pill is gone; its entry now lives in the empty state, overflow and Settings.

DESIGN NOTES
• One list, two zones: DOWNLOADING pinned on top, finished files under day headers. No tabs, no separate queue screen.
• A failed row states the remedy, not the error: "Link expired — reopen the page", and its right control becomes retry.
• Percent lives on the thumbnail as white-on-scrim, so the metadata line stays free for bytes and speed.
• Unwatched dot sits left of the title and disappears after first play; partially watched files carry a 3dp bar at the thumbnail bottom.
• Search happens in-header — the title morphs into a field, list filters live, no second screen.
• Layout toggle swaps its own icon (grid ⟷ list) and is the only control that changes state on tap without a sheet.
