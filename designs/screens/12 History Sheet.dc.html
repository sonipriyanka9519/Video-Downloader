DESIGN NOTES
• 90% height, not full screen: the page stays visible behind it, so the sheet reads as a lookup you can dismiss.
• Consecutive visits to one URL collapse into a single row with a ×N chip, which is what keeps a day's list readable.
• Search filters live inside the sheet and reports a result count — no separate screen, no empty transition.
• Each row has its own X for a single delete; the only bulk action is Clear all, behind a confirm naming the real count.
• Retention is offered once, inside the first Clear all — a pre-ticked "clear older than 30 days automatically" that then lives in Settings. No separate onboarding for it.
• Private browsing writes nothing here, so this screen needs no private filter and no explanation.
DECIDED
History keeps everything by default. The first Clear all asks once whether to auto-clear anything older than 30 days; the answer becomes a Settings row.
