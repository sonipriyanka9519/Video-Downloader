SCREEN 04
Search / address screen
A full screen, not a dropdown — the browser is told nothing until the user commits, so backing out can never change the page. Keyboard opens with it; every row is either a destination or a way to fill the field without leaving.
Assumptions: keyboard is a neutral placeholder for the system IME. No voice search, no QR scanner. Non-URL input falls back to the search engine set in Settings — never exposed here.

DESIGN NOTES
• Two row types, two behaviours: history and page rows go; search rows carry a ↖ insert arrow that only fills the field.
• The copied-link card shows a middle-ellipsised URL with an eye to reveal the whole thing — tapping the card goes. A declined link is never offered again.
• Arriving from a page: the full URL is prefilled and fully selected, so the first keystroke replaces it; the current page appears as its own "THIS PAGE" row.
• Every history row has an X to delete just that entry; "Clear history" sits at the bottom of the empty state behind a confirm dialog.
• Nothing is committed until Enter or a row tap — backing out leaves the browser exactly as it was.
• Empty field shows at most 6 recent searches and 6 recently visited, deduplicated by URL.
