DESIGN NOTES
• Three channels: Progress (silent, ongoing, cannot be swiped away), Complete (default priority, the only one that makes a sound), Reminders (low, ≤2/week, off until the user opts in).
• Every notification carries its remedy as an action — Use mobile data, Reopen page — rather than sending the user into the app to find out what went wrong.
• Concurrent downloads collapse into one summary line with Pause all, so three downloads never mean three ongoing notifications.
• Completion uses the thumbnail as the large icon; that image is the fastest way to recognise which download finished.
• Widget mirrors the app's card language but keeps system margins and never restyles the shade — no custom notification layouts.
• Reminders are off by default. Once three downloads sit unwatched, the library offers them once, in place — a notification never arrives unasked.
DECIDED
Unwatched reminders are opt-in, offered from the Downloads screen once three items sit unwatched — panel D shows that offer. The shade card in panel A is what an opted-in user sees.
