SCREEN 08
Collections
Lightweight folders that are never mandatory — a download never asks for one. The same sheet does two jobs: filter the library, or file a selection. One level deep, no nesting, no colour labels; the cover is simply the newest item's thumbnail.
Assumptions: creating a collection is inline in the sheet, not a dialog. Deleting a collection never deletes videos — the confirm says so in those words.

DESIGN NOTES
• One sheet, two modes: from the chip it filters (tap closes and filters); from a selection it files (checkboxes plus one primary button).
• Creating is inline — the row becomes a field with a Create button. No dialog, no new screen, no naming step forced on anyone.
• A collection filter shows as a dismissible chip at the head of the chip row, so the user can always see why the list is short.
• Collections are a filter, not a tab. Detail view is just the library list scoped to one name, with rename/delete in the overflow.
• Deleting a collection never deletes videos, and the confirm says exactly that with the real count.
• Cover art is the newest item's thumbnail — nothing to choose, nothing to maintain.
